## Art generation

Engine: MiniMax image-01 (`~/AI/SUCCESSION/staged/ccbync-swap/pilot/gen_image.py`), per house
standing rule. Cost: 4 successful generations (1 satellite prompt was regenerated once after the
first attempt came back as a spacecraft engine close-up instead of a distant silhouette — 5 calls
total). MiniMax billing is per-call flat rate; no per-token cost tracked separately.

**Seamless tiling approach** (per the mission's explicit guidance): the BG (base, full-coverage)
layer is **fully procedural** — numpy/Pillow periodic value-noise (wraps exactly at the lattice
boundary) + modulo-wrapped star scatter (any star near an edge is placed with wraparound so it
can't be half-cut at a seam). This is tileable *by construction*, not by post-hoc patching. The
Neb (nebula) and Sat (satellite silhouette) layers are MiniMax renders — not tileable — so instead
of trying to force-tile a hero image, they're processed with a **luminance + vignette alpha mask**:
black background pixels become transparent, and all four edges fade to alpha=0 within a margin.
Since alpha=0 meets alpha=0 at every wrap boundary regardless of RGB content, the tile-boundary seam
is a non-issue by construction for these two layers, and the seamless procedural BG shows through
underneath. This is a stronger guarantee than blur/clone-stamp patching.

### Seam verification (measured, not asserted)

Method: `wrap_diff()` compares mean absolute difference between opposite edge columns/rows,
alpha-weighted (0 = perfectly seamless, out of a 0-255 scale). 2x2 tile-preview PNGs rendered for
eyeball check, included in `CONTACT-SHEET.png` (this directory) and generated at
`/tmp/.../parallax/{proc,processed}/*_tilepreview.png` during the work (not copied into the repo —
see contact sheet for the visual evidence).

## Candidates (see `CONTACT-SHEET.png`)

1. **Corporate Reach — RECOMMENDED, wired.** Deep near-black void, thin wispy acid-green nebula
   veins (restrained, not floodlight), plus a distant angular PROVIDENCE-satellite silhouette
   glimpsed against faint green haze. Best match to the brief: "vast, cold, corporate-owned space;
   faint acid-green nebula veins... distant PROVIDENCE-satellite silhouette layer (subtle,
   non-cartoonish)."
2. **Void Ledger — alternate.** Even more restrained — the nebula veins are thinner and the Neb
   foreground layer is nearly imperceptible. No satellite layer. Good if "faint" needs to be pushed
   further; arguably under-delivers on "wow."
3. **Signal Bloom — alternate.** Boldest and brightest of the three: denser veins, a swirling nebula
   with a bright star core in the Neb layer. Visually the most striking single image, but risks
   reading as floodlight rather than restrained corporate menace — the brand-check line explicitly
   calls for "accents not floodlight." Kept as an alternate for if the owner wants more drama.

Alternates (Void Ledger, Signal Bloom) are staged under `alternates/<name>/` in this directory —
BG + Neb PNGs only, not wired into any prototype, not part of the committed game asset tree.
