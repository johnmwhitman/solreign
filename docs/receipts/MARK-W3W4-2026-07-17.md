### The C6 boundary decision (the W2 receipt's flagged debt)

**Decision: C6 is a payload SUBSTITUTION inside the two existing beat channels, not a third
channel.** When a spawn's nudge or growth beat is due for an account whose `slot_index >=
solreign.mark.slots` (a pure row-vs-CVar comparison — no ECS query, so this system stays disjoint
from the garden system and cannot disagree with the projection's dense-slot seniority law, spec §9
Q3), the beat's TEXT becomes a C6 overflow line — but it still consumes the SAME write-first stamp
(`nudge_shown`, or the `last_visit_stage` conditional advance) the un-overflowed line would have.

**Justification:** (1) The task brief's cap — "at most 4 lines ever per account (W1's
TryMarkNudgeShownAsync/TryRecordVisitAsync stamps)" — names the stamps as the cap's mechanism; a
free-standing un-stamped C6 channel ("every spawn while overflowed") would blow that budget
unboundedly for any account that stays overflowed. (2) Spec §3.3.4's own words are substitutional:
overflow owners "get the C6 overflow line on their next spawn **instead of the return beat**".
(3) Spec §6 counts the total as "at most 4 times EVER per account (3 stage advances + 1 nudge)" —
a reading that adds C6 events on top would contradict the spec's own interplay analysis. The cost
of this reading — an overflowed account's mark can silently re-materialize when capacity frees up,
with no line on that spawn if its stamps are spent — is accepted: the examine surface is always
current, and at pop 1-5 overflow is years away (24 default slots).
